Hi everyone, I’m Daulet, and this is Speakline. It’s Team 2’s work: Asylbek built the payments, Altair the numbers, and I’ll walk you through it.

0:11  2 · Problem
Quick question: who here has ever rehearsed an interview answer in the shower? Yeah. Your interview is on Thursday, and you already know you’ll freeze and say “um”. You’re not alone: 93 percent of Americans get anxious before a job interview, and one in three fears public speaking. People literally ask for help: “I need a legit plan to get better. Do any AI coaches actually work?”

0:40  3 · Audience & market
So who is this for? Any US adult with an important talk and a date on the calendar: an interview, a pitch, a call. That’s 63 million hires a year. If just one percent buy, that’s about 61 million dollars a year. And people already pay: Vocal Image makes 12 million a year, Speak over 100 million.

1:04  4 · Competitors
So why can we win? Look at the gaps. Vocal Image trains your voice, but not for a date. RiseGuide runs fourteen thousand US ads, but reviews say there’s no live speaking, and the price jumps. We do one thing: live practice plus a plan to your date, sold on the web.

1:26  5 · Product (video 87 s)

3:05  6 · How we earn
How do we make money? We sell on the web, so no 30 percent app-store cut. A trial week for 9.99, then 29.99 a month, or 59.99 for three months, the one we preselect. And the price never jumps at renewal.

3:23  7 · Numbers
Now the big question: does it pay? We stayed cautious, US only. One payer costs us 67 dollars 50 in ads and brings back 82 50 in the first year. That’s 1.22, paid back in month 7. Why? Ads cost 30 dollars per thousand views, we get 1.8 percent clicks, just under the median, and since our prices went up, we assume 15 percent fewer people pay. Can it be better? Yes: strong hooks take us to 1.36, and if people stay longer, 1.42. The risk is weak static ads: 0.82.

4:02  8 · How we get there
So how do we get there? Three steps. First, creatives: static and video together, 60 to 40. Static sells cheaper, video brings the clicks. That test costs 4,400 dollars and two weeks. Second, the paywall: your result and your plan right on top. Third, retention: the calendar reminds you before every meeting, so there’s always a next reason to practise. We need about 12 thousand dollars to break even by month 14, and we scale only at 1.5.

4:35  9 · Creatives
And our creatives start from the pain, not features. Statics go after the nerves: “Heart pounding before every big talk?” “You know your stuff. Your nerves don’t.” Videos show the symptom: the “um”, the interview on Thursday. Every ad ends with the free 10-second test.
