Cancellation and refunds

Last updated: [publication date]

This page forms part of the Terms of Service. Cancelling should be as easy as subscribing.

1. How to cancel

  1. In the app: Settings → Subscription → “Manage subscription” → “Cancel subscription”.
  2. Or via the link in your receipt email from [payment partner].
  3. Or email [support@domain] from your account address.

After cancelling you keep Premium until the end of the paid period, and you will not be charged again.

2. Trial week

The trial week costs $4.99. Unless you cancel before the end of day 7, it renews as a monthly plan at $17.99. We email you a reminder 2 days before the first full charge.

3. Refunds

  • First payment on any plan — full refund if you ask within 7 days.
  • An unexpected renewal — full refund if you ask within 48 hours of the charge and haven’t used Premium since.
  • Outage — if the Service was unavailable for more than 3 days in a row, a refund for the days without access.
  • “Meeting prep” pack — full refund within 7 days if you opened no more than 2 of its scenarios.

Refunds go back to the same card within 10 working days; the exact timing depends on your bank.

4. Contact

[support@domain] — we reply within 3 working days.
