2. Voice and microphone

  • The microphone is on only while you record, after you tap the button.
  • Loudness, pauses and intonation are computed on your device. We do not store your audio or send it to our servers.
  • Speech-to-text uses your browser’s built-in recognition. In Google Chrome the audio is sent to Google’s recognition service under Google’s privacy policy.
  • The detailed AI review (Pro) sends the recognised text and metrics to Anthropic (Claude) to generate tips. Audio is not sent.

3. Payments

Payments are handled by [payment partner]. Card details are entered in its secure form and never reach us. We receive the payment confirmation, plan, dates and the last 4 digits of the card.

4. Where data is stored

Personal data is stored on servers located in the Republic of Kazakhstan: [hosting provider and data centre]. Until you create an account, practice results stay only in your browser.

5. Analytics and cookies

To see where people drop off, the app sends anonymised events to our server (opened the app, finished onboarding, recorded, viewed plans, paid) with a random browser identifier, without your name or email. After you sign in, events are linked to your account. The Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel for ad measurement is enabled only after you accept it in the cookie banner.

6. Sharing

We do not sell data. We share it only with services the app needs: the payment partner, hosting, the email service for sign-in codes, Meta (only with your ad-cookie consent) and, if you use the AI review, Anthropic. Cross-border transfers happen only with your consent.

7. Retention

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of a deletion request. Payment records are kept for the periods required by Kazakhstan tax law.

8. Your rights

You can access, correct or withdraw consent for your data by writing to [privacy@domain]; we stop processing within 15 working days of a withdrawal. You can delete your account and data yourself in Settings → “Delete account and data”; this also cancels your subscription.
